<objective>
Close CR-05 and the four context-adapter warnings (WR-18 to WR-21). Purpose: a hostile repository's planning files and indexes cannot stall AVARICE or speak to the agent as trusted text (FR-14, FR-22, CON-11).
Output: linear heading parser with a line cap, confined refresh, depth-independent excludes, consistent untrusted marking.
