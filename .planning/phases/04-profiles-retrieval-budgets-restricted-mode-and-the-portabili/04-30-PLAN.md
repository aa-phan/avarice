<objective>
Resource bounds with defined degradation (OPS-5, D-13, CON-12). Bounds are constants scattered through the code (PACK_BUDGET 4,000, RUN_PROMPT_MAX_BYTES, no quota, no run limit), and the profile config is not read for any of them. This plan parses the limits section with defaults, raises the pack budget to the 24,000 ceiling and resolves it against the 32 KiB prompt, and makes every bound degrade as defined: drop and list, queue, truncate, refuse with a message. It also wires pack reuse on retry through the CON-12 rule.

Purpose: SC2 bounds and D-13's recorded resolution.
Output: domain bounds module, item, context, start, checks, artifact store, ledger and doctor changes, tests.
</objective>
