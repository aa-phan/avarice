<objective>
Close three warnings on profile isolation and credential handling (WR-15, WR-16, WR-17). The profile registry skips a profile config it could not read, so that profile's moved roots leave the sandbox deny list; two processes binding repositories at once can lose a binding, which lets a bound repository open under another profile; and the cmd: provider rejects a good credential when its helper writes a lot to stderr.

Purpose: SEC-1 (no cross-profile access, no fallback) and SEC-2 (credentials resolved reliably by reference) hold under errors and concurrency.
Output: fail-closed registry, locked bindings writes, stdout-only credential size check.
</objective>
