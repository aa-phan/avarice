<objective>
Parameter-bound approvals (FR-12, FR-13, FR-15, ADR-005). The approvals table exists but has no writer or reader. This plan adds the parameter digest and key rules, ledger methods that consume an approval in the transaction that starts the action, the `authorizeAction` gate with the contract source and the policy evaluator, the `avarice approve` owner command with revoke, and the no-replay rule on resume.

Purpose: SC3 "a privileged action without a matching parameter-bound approval is denied, approving a plan does not approve its actions"; promotion (plan 04-21) and the hook (plan 04-19) call this gate.
Output: domain rules, ledger methods, app gate, CLI command, tests.
</objective>
