<objective>
Build the evaluation-harness side of G-04-3 and close GIN-04. G-04-3: an AC-8 success counted runs that skipped part of their brief, because the brief text told every run that an end-to-end test it asked for was out of scope and the judge looked only at the checks and the held-out test. This plan adds per-run brief coverage to the harness's judgement (pure, read-only, no spend), records the amended success rule apart from the pinned task list, and stops the brief from forbidding work it asks for. GIN-04: `stepSpendUsd` charged the whole cap to a step that exited before any host started.

Purpose: plan 04-63 can record the coverage, get the owner's re-approval and re-judge from recorded data (FR-22, the owner decision on G-04-3); the Phase 4 ceiling is not consumed by spend that never happened.
Output: harness functions and their unit tests.
</objective>
