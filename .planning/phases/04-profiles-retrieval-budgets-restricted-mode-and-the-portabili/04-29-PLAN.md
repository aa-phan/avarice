<objective>
Retrieval limits on the agent's own searches (CON-10, CON-11, S-16, CS-16). Plan 04-28 caps AVARICE's context assembly; the agent inside a unit can still search without a shared retrieval budget, and an unfiltered whole-repository search needs no authorization. This plan puts the contract's retrieval limits into the hook policy, charges every Grep and Glob to retrieval.searches with the existing atomic reservation, and gates unfiltered whole-repository searches on retrieval.broadDiscovery.

Purpose: S-16's "summed counters never exceed their limits" for the agent and its subagents.
Output: hook policy, decision and entry changes, tests including a parallel race.
</objective>
