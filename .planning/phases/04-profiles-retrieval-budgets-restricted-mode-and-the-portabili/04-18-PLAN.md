<objective>
Transcript and artifact boundaries (SEC-3, D-14). The transcript is redacted only after the 20 MiB cap (a split secret survives), whole transcripts are always kept, and other text artifacts (context packs, manual evidence, repair records) are stored as given. This plan redacts the transcript line by line before the cap, keeps a redacted summary plus a digest by default with whole transcripts only on opt-in, and wraps the artifact store so every text kind is redacted before storage.

Purpose: AC-5 (0 secret patterns in artifacts) and the "no full payloads by default" half of SEC-3.
Output: host adapter changes, retention parse, artifact decorator, composition wiring, tests.
</objective>
