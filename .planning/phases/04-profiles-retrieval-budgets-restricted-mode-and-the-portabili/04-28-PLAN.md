<objective>
Retrieval caps, shared counters and cache scope (CON-10, CON-11, CON-12, D-09). The assembler has a token budget but no per-step caps, no broad-discovery rule, no persisted counters and no cache scope. This plan adds the cap table as profile defaults that a contract may only lower, a guard that reserves retrieval counters atomically in budget rows, adapter-level enforcement of scope, result, byte, timeout, cancellation and page limits, and the rule that a cached item is never reused after revocation, scope change or across profiles.

Purpose: SC2 "context retrieval is handle-first and capped".
Output: domain caps and cache rule, contract keys, guard, adapter enforcement, tests including a race.
</objective>
