<objective>
Close CR-01 and CR-02 of the attended approval gate (SC3, FR-12). Today the privileged-command classifier returns "nothing privileged" for a line past its length, nesting or wrapper limits, and loses the rest of a line after a double-quoted `)` inside `$(...)`; the attended hook then prints nothing and the host runs the command. This plan makes classification three-valued (none, privileged, unparseable), denies every unparseable line in the attended hook, and replaces the splitter with a scanner that follows the shell's quoting, comment, here-document and redirection rules, checked against bash itself.

Purpose: "A privileged action without a matching parameter-bound approval is denied" must hold for any line the hook cannot read.
Output: classifyLine and its tri-state type, the attended and run consumers, the scanner, and the differential oracle test.
</objective>
