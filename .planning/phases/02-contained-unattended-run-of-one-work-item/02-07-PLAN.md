|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-02-18 | Tampering | hostile objects or a ref update in the user's repository (fetchIntoSource) | high | mitigate | fsck on fetch; create-only ref with equal/different check; no `+`; no checkout; digest test of HEAD, index, tree, refs |
