## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-02-49 | Spoofing | escape sequences in decision issues or paths rewriting the terminal | medium | mitigate | stripControl on every model-derived or repository-derived string; tested in inbox |

| T-02-52 | Information disclosure | status reading model output from transcripts | low | mitigate | status reads ledger rows only (IDs, statuses, digests); transcripts are not opened |
| T-02-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | high | mitigate | No package installs in this plan |
