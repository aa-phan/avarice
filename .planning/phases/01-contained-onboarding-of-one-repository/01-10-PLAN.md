## Phase Goal

**As a** solo developer, **I want to** onboard one existing repository into AVARICE, **so that** later unattended runs start from an active project record with a code-and-document graph.

Purpose: the record is what later runs trust. It must say exactly what was mapped, at which revision, how completely, and must never carry authority that repository text proposed (ONB-7, ONB-9), and AVARICE must hold references and digests, not a second copy of GSD's knowledge (ONB-2).
Output: validation rules, secret patterns, path-reference check, narrowing, completed record builder, durable snapshot.
</objective>
