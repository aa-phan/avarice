## Phase Goal

**As a** solo developer, **I want to** onboard one existing repository into AVARICE, **so that** later unattended runs start from an active project record with a code-and-document graph.

Purpose: GSD is the semantic mapper (ONB-2, ADR-008); AVARICE must find the right GSD, refuse the wrong one (ONB-12, S-36), keep commits off (ONB-5) and record mode and completeness GSD does not (ONB-7). Research Pitfall 1 (GSD not found under a split config dir) and Pitfall 5 (`config-set` hangs inside the sandbox) are designed out here.
Output: GSD port and adapter, composition wiring, staging configuration, mapping mode table.
</objective>
