<objective>
`claude -p --resume` for an answered item (D-08, D-16, SC3). Plan 03-18 runs an answered item as a fresh unit; this plan puts the resumed session in front of that path: unpark the item's delta, resume its own GSD session with the answer, and fall back to the fresh unit when the session is gone, the resume crashes or the delta cannot be unparked. Resume after a supervisor kill reconciles a resumed attempt like any other.

Purpose: the paused GSD session continues where it asked, as SC3 states, with a safe fallback.
Output: item-stage resume path, invocation option, reconcile and kill-point coverage, two test files.
</objective>
