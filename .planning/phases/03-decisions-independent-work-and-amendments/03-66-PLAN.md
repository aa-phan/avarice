<assumption_delta_decision>
The phase-scope detector fires on roadmap wording ("second", "another", "fallback", "supplementary", "optional"). For this plan the pluralized noun is the epoch. This plan adds a second, per-run epoch for the stop-loss count, begun by a confirmed reset. Noun now primary for the stop-loss: the run's stop-loss epoch (`stopLossEpochOf(evidence, role)`). Accepted debt: two epoch notions in one tool, kept apart by name and by invariant tests (Task 3). A later decision that tied the pass-validity epoch to a reset would force a promote.
