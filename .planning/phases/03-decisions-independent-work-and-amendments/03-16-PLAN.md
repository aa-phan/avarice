<objective>
Local notification (D-21, D-22, D-24, AUT-4, AUT-6). Today a blocked run is visible only to someone who runs `inbox`. This plan adds the Notifier port, the osascript adapter with argv-only text, the profile config reader, and the app service that records every notification and its delivery attempts in the ledger, holds banners in quiet hours, and never touches a decision. The digest command (plan 03-23) and the batching flush at item boundaries (plan 03-18) reuse it.

Purpose: SC2 offline: a failing notifier leaves the decision open and visible; a working one tells the owner without model text.
Output: port, adapter, config reader, app service, composition and supervise wiring, tests.
</objective>
